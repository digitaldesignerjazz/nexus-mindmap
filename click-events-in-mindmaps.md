# Click Events in Mindmaps

You can make mindmap nodes clickable:

```mermaid
mindmap
  root((NEXUS))
    AI
    Mesh

click AI "https://github.com/digitaldesignerjazz/nexus-mindmap" "Explore the AI Layer"
click Mesh "https://github.com/digitaldesignerjazz/mermaid-diagram-syntax" "Advanced Mesh techniques"
```

**Note:** Click events work best when the mindmap is rendered in Mermaid Live, GitHub, or custom web pages. They are excellent for turning the mindmap into a navigation hub.
